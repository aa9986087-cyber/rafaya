# «قلعة مارد» in the modern world of al-Jouf (the owner's plan, 27 Nov 2026)

Nothing is built yet. Decided with the owner, one question at a time.

- **The goal:** one castle in the middle of the al-Jouf map, fought over by everyone. Its holder is «أمير الجوف».
- **When:** open to attack at any time (no window).
- **Who may attack:** any town that has the military command centre (any stage, small or big).
- **The holder gets no money:** a crown over his flag on the map, a special badge beside his name every time he
  writes in the chat (to tease the others), and the record: the all-time list of the longest holders.
- **Foot soldiers only, no missiles or drones at all** in the castle fight. Missiles stay for wars between towns.
- **Three kinds of soldiers:** مشاة (cheap, many), قوات خاصة (costly, strongest attacking), قناصة (strongest defending from the walls).
- **The march takes 5 minutes**, the same from anywhere on the map.
- **The fallen are gone; survivors return** to their camp, or become the castle's garrison if they won it.
- Proposed (not yet confirmed): a new «معسكر الجنود» trains infantry (money + time, capacity grows with the camp and
  the town); «ازحف على قلعة مارد» sends the army walking across the world map, seen by all, with an alert to everyone
  and the holder; the march takes the same time from anywhere; the server decides the fight (numbers, upgrades);
  the battle is shown in 3D (modern soldiers, Rocketbox Military avatars, MIT); one hour of protection after a capture;
  honour points for every attacker; titles «أمير الجوف» and «سيّد الجوف» (most time in total).
