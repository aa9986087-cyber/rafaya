# The end of the modern world of al-Jouf, and «الجوف القديمة» (the owner's plan, 27 Nov 2026)

**When the owner says «جاء وقت النهاية» (or asks to start the ending), remind them of this plan and start it.**
No date is set yet: the owner chooses it then. Nothing here is built yet.

## Decided by the owner
- A **new world beside the current one**: «الجوف القديمة», 600 years back (soldiers, archers, horsemen, castles).
  The current world is not wiped.
- The current world **must have an ending** (below).
- **تاج الجوف الأخير** goes to the town with the **most war victories** during the last month.
- **Everyone moves at once** (decided 27 Nov 2026): right after the ending cinematic every player goes to
  «الجوف القديمة»; the modern world becomes «متحف الجوف» at the same moment (no period with both worlds open).
- The trailer is approved (v5, 27 Nov 2026), with the title «الجوف القديمة» only (no «قريبًا»): it plays
  before everyone moves.
- Alliances (for the coming worlds): **anyone may ally with anyone, two towns per alliance only**.
- Also discussed before (not yet decided): after الحاضرة الكبرى a «دولة» stage (suggested: 40,000 people,
  held 3 jouf days, at most 3 states), reputation, protection of small towns by a state, a council of states.

## The ending as proposed (the owner agreed to its shape)
1. **سر قلعة مارد**, a month before the end: the bulletin announces an ancient gate found under Qasr Marid.
   Every town may give money and materials to build it; a big counter shows how far it is.
2. **The last war** in the same month: the crown «تاج الجوف الأخير» for the most war victories in that month,
   written into the game's history for good.
3. **The last day**: when the gate is complete, a cinematic (a sandstorm covers al-Jouf, the gate opens, white),
   and each player gets a certificate (town, highest population, medals, rank).
4. **متحف الجوف** afterwards: the modern world is frozen, never deleted; anyone may visit any town, nobody may
   build or fight. A roll of honour: the last king, the biggest town, the medal holders, the gate's top givers.
5. **Into الجوف القديمة**: everyone starts equal from a tent. Rewards are for show only, never strength:
   the last king starts with a golden banner and «سليل الملوك»; the gate's top givers «فاتح البوابة»;
   every old player «من أهل الجوف الأولين» beside their name.

## The ancient world: the full plan
The owner's full plan page (Arabic): `docs/ancient-world.html`, published at https://claude.ai/artifact/7M5BJNmgUc7D9Nqsus3kn1.

Decided on 27 Nov 2026: battles in the style of the well-known base-attack games (the attacker drops troops
around the defender's own town and they fight by themselves; defences fight automatically), and the defender may
join live with a few limited actions. The goal of the game: the great castle in the middle of the map (Qasr
Marid); its holder is «أمير الجوف»; the castle **never ends** (no eras, no reset): an all-time record ranks everyone by total time holding it
(the main contest), with the longest single reign, most captures, most defences and the castle's history; the
all-time leader carries «سيّد الجوف» until someone passes them.
All names, art and units are our own (no copying any existing game).

Also decided: the ancient world runs on **real time** (not the 20-minute jouf day); everything takes time to
build; a slow, rewarding pace (2 builders at start, up to 5; build times grow with level; a stage needs people,
a key building and a minimum time in the stage before; no paid speed-ups; capped treasury). Lessons from the
modern world: أبو يعقوب reached الحاضرة الكبرى in days. Stage names approved: مضرب، قرية، بلدة، مدينة مسوّرة، إمارة، الإمارة الكبرى.
Cinematics: the owner subscribes to an Arabic voice service (name to confirm, likely ElevenLabs) for the
transition scenes' narration; its key goes in through the secure credential form, never in chat or the repo.

## Armies, food and trade (the owner, 27 Nov 2026)
- Armies you raise and lead, in the style of the base-attack genre: each unit kind is trained and counted,
  and the army needs **room** (camps whose capacity grows as they are upgraded), so a bigger army needs more upgrades.
- **Food** is a real resource: farms, palms, wells and herds make it; soldiers and horses eat it.
- **Trade between players**: buying and selling goods between towns (a market).
- All names and art our own; nothing copied from any existing game.

### Decided one by one (the owner, 27 Nov 2026)
1. **One resource: gold** (like the modern world). The army's food is paid in gold (an upkeep), not a second resource.
2. **Troops sent into a battle are spent**, whether they live or die; troops not sent go home (the base-attack way).
3. **Protection after being attacked, by the loss**: about half the town destroyed → 12 hours; all of it → 16 hours; a small loss → none. Attacking someone yourself ends your own protection.
4. **The winner takes gold and honour**: loot up to about 20% of the defender's gold (the treasury keeps part safe), and honour points that raise the winner's rank and lower the loser's.
5. **The market between players is for mercenaries**: a town trains soldiers and hires them out or sells them to another town, for gold, for one battle.
6. **How mercenaries work**: the seller lists trained soldiers at a price; the buyer gets them for **one attack only**. A minimum price stops cheap sales to an ally or a second account.
7. **Who may attack whom**: only towns of the same stage, or one stage above or below. The big cannot crush the small; the small may challenge one step up.
8. **Qasr Marid is open to anyone from the start** (the stage rule above does not apply to it), but its defence is very strong.
9. **Who defends the castle**: its walls and towers come with the game; its garrison is the emir's soldiers and his ally's, and both may defend live while it is attacked.
10. **Units, all four groups**: footman (sword and shield), archer, spearman (strong against horse); horseman and camel rider (sturdier, carries more loot); siege weapons in later stages (ram for gates, ladders for walls, catapult); and one hero commander per player, upgraded and named, who strengthens the troops near him and rests a while when worn out.
11. **Control in battle**: drop troops from any side around the enemy town and they fight by themselves; tap a building to send a group at it; the hero has a special strike you trigger yourself.
12. **Live defence, three limited actions**: send out the garrison waiting in the camp, pour boiling oil from the wall once, and use the hero's strike. It helps but cannot turn a battle alone.
